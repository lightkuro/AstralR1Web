# Astral Web

**A first-person rhythm shooter that runs in your browser.**

Run down a neon city highway with a rifle in hand. Glowing boxes fly in from the distance, and you shoot them right on the beat.

Astral Web is an alpha demo of [Astral Runshot](https://lightkuro.itch.io/astral-r1). It plays the same songs as the full game, and you can bring in your own osu! beatmaps too.

> **Alpha demo.** Feedback is very welcome: [lightkuro.itch.io/astral-r1](https://lightkuro.itch.io/astral-r1)

---

## How to play

Targets fly toward you and reach the hit line on the beat. A square frame closes in around each one; shoot when it locks on.

- **Magenta boxes:** single shots. One click each.
- **Cyan boxes:** holds. Keep the trigger down and track the box as it rides along its path.
- **Yellow and red boxes** (optional): stacked targets that take two or three hits.

Your timing is judged on every shot, from **Critical Perfect** down to a miss. Holds are graded **LOCKED**, **GREAT**, **GOOD** or **BROKEN**. Chain hits to build your combo and push your accuracy toward **101%**, with ranks from **D** up to **SSS+**.

### Controls

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Aim | Mouse (or touch) |
| Shoot | Left click, `Z`, `X`, `J`, `K` (hold on holds) |
| Change lane | `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` |
| Pause | `Esc`, `P` |
| Continue / quit (paused) | `Space` / `Esc` |
| Retry | `R` |
| Play (menu) | `Enter` |

On phones and tablets, just tap (and hold) to shoot.

## Features

### Songs
- **Neon Nowhere**, the built-in demo song, on Normal and Hard.
- **Play songs from Astral Runshot.** Open any song folder from the full game and play it straight away.
- **Import osu! beatmaps.** Pick a `.osz` file or drop it onto the page. All four osu! modes convert, including sliders (as holds you track along a curved path), spinners and kiai lighting. Imported maps are saved in your browser so you can come back to them.

### Aiming
- **Mouse look** (default on computers): the view turns with your mouse and the crosshair stays centred. Click to lock the pointer.
- **Free cursor** (default on phones): aim wherever you point or tap.

### The run
- A neon city at night: a dark road with glowing lane lines and edge rails, lit towers and violet fog.
- Songs change pace and mood as they play, with speed shifts, flashes, colour changes and FOV kicks.
- Rifle recoil and tracers on every shot.

### Settings
Tune the game to how you like to play, mostly live from the pause menu:

- **Aim and shooting:** aim mode, sensitivity, hold-to-keep-firing, muzzle flash, beam snapping on holds
- **Targets:** size, double and triple boxes (shown as a stack or a single box)
- **Timing:** Strict, Standard, Lenient or Relaxed windows; fly-in speed; how long targets stay visible; audio offset
- **Crosshair:** cross or dot, size, thickness, gap and colour, with a live preview
- **Audio:** music and effect volumes
- **Graphics:** High (with glow) or Low

**Autoplay** and **No Fail** (on by default) are on the song panel. Your settings and best scores are saved in your browser.
